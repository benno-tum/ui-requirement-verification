> Alessio Ferrari, Giorgio Oronzo Spagnolo, and Stefania Gnesi. *PURE: a

merge` and `2010 - mashboot`, contextualizing extracted statements, decomposing
requirements into claims, assigning UI-evaluability and verification labels,
linking evidence to document figures, and calculating exploratory results. The

this derivative work.
